# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - 2026-09-27

First public release.

- **Library** (`engthermo`, 18 modules): IAPWS-IF97 steam; 32 components with sourced data; ideal gases and
  mixtures; refrigerant tables and cycles; virial and cubic equations of state with departure functions and
  mixtures; vapour pressure; mixture properties; Margules, van Laar, Wilson, NRTL, UNIQUAC and UNIFAC; low- and
  high-pressure VLE; liquid-liquid (incl. Flory-Huggins) and solid-liquid equilibria; reaction equilibrium and
  Gibbs minimisation; combustion and Ellingham diagrams; electrochemistry; 11 bundled data files.
- **Course**: 18 executed notebooks with learning objectives, "Inside the algorithm" sections and exercises, and
  18 notebooks of worked solutions (54 exercises).
- **Validation**: 150 checks against IAPWS-IF97, CoolProp, iapws, thermo, textbook examples, exact identities
  and experimental values.
